About Kitov

Where every girl’s flame is given room to grow.

Kitov Sachra is a Bais Yaakov seminary for young women after high school. Our mission is simple: to help each student reach the highest caliber of spiritual and personal growth, in a warm and loving environment.

The year is lived in Yerushalayim itself, in the Geula neighborhood, minutes from the Old City and the Kosel. Classes, chesed, trips and friendships all happen in the city that sits at the center of our tefillos.

The year

More than a year abroad.

A seminary year is a turning point. At Kitov it is built on four things.

01

Learning

Daily classes in Torah, hashkafa and halacha, taught in English by teachers who know each student and are there for her.

02

Chesed

Giving is part of the week, including regular visits with children with special needs in Jerusalem: art, dancing, and cards for the Yamim Tovim.

03

Eretz Yisrael

Trips across the land that are meant to be meaningful, not just memorable, from the Kosel to the north and the south.

04

Friendship & production

A close-knit family of girls, and a big seminary production that brings the whole year together on one stage.

Questions

Good to know

Don’t see your question? Request access and our office will answer it personally.

Who is Kitov Sachra for?

Young women after high school who want a year of learning and growth in Jerusalem. Classes are in English, and the program lists ages 17 to 25.

Where is the seminary?

In the Geula neighborhood of Jerusalem, in the center of the city.

How long is the program?

The seminary year runs 8 to 10 months.

Where do students live?

Students live together in seminary dormitory housing.

Is Kitov a Masa program?

Yes. Kitov Sachra is listed as a Masa Israel program. Eligibility for Masa grants is set by Masa, so please check with them for your situation.

Can I earn college credit?

Kitov Sachra is a participating seminary in Touro University’s Israel Option. Credit for the year is recorded if you go on to attend a participating Touro college.

How do I apply?

Start by requesting access. Our office will reach out with everything you need to know.
